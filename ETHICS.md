# Ethical Framework

## Purpose

This repository forms part of a non-clinical infrastructure designed to support
humans when cognitive capacity, memory, or executive function is impaired.

The ethical framework defined here governs all present and future systems built
on top of this infrastructure, including:

- Sprer’n (Norway)
- AGI Brain Assist™ (international standard, via BrainSays.com)

---

## Core Ethical Principle

**When cognitive capacity is reduced, responsibility must shift from the
individual to the system.**

This infrastructure exists to reduce cognitive and emotional load —
not to evaluate, optimize, or discipline human behavior.

---

## Non-Clinical Boundary

This system is explicitly non-clinical.

It does not:
- diagnose medical conditions
- provide treatment or therapy
- replace healthcare professionals
- make medical decisions

All support is functional, structural, and assistive.

---

## Human Dignity by Design

The system must:
- reduce complexity
- minimize required choices
- avoid urgency-based pressure
- preserve user dignity under reduced capacity

Lack of action must never be interpreted as resistance, failure, or non-compliance.

---

## Data Minimization

The infrastructure follows strict data minimization principles:

- No personal health data is required at the infrastructure layer
- No behavioral profiling is performed
- No persistent identity is required

Support must be possible without personal disclosure.

---

## Zero-Knowledge Alignment

The system is designed to be compatible with Zero Knowledge (ZK) principles.

This enables:
- verification without disclosure
- accountability without surveillance
- system-level validation without user exposure

The system may verify that support occurred —
without knowing content, cause, or personal context.

---

## No Behavioral Manipulation

The system must not:
- coerce or nudge users toward productivity
- score, rank, or gamify behavior
- penalize inactivity
- enforce compliance through incentives

Human vulnerability must never be monetized or exploited.

---

## Health-Economy Constraints

Any health-economy mechanisms built on this infrastructure must:

- create value through stabilization, not extraction
- operate at system level, not individual exposure
- remain compatible with zero-knowledge validation
- avoid dependency creation

Economic logic must never override human dignity.

---

## Transparency & Accountability

While personal data is minimized, system behavior must remain auditable.

This includes:
- transparent infrastructure logic
- clear separation of system layers
- explicit documentation of non-clinical scope

---

## Governance

This ethical framework is a living document.

Any future extension must preserve:
- non-clinical classification
- zero-knowledge compatibility
- human-centered design under reduced cognitive capacity

---

## Summary

This infrastructure exists to support humans
when thinking becomes too heavy to carry alone.

Ethics is not an add-on.
It is enforced through architecture.
